FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION. This file is invented and is not scientific evidence.

Proposed study
We will test whether two lighting schedules change the growth of an invented
laboratory plant. Independently assigned growth trays would be the experimental
units; repeated photographs of the same tray would not be independent replicates.
We propose to compare a prespecified growth measurement between schedules.

Unresolved inputs
The number of independent trays, achievable precision, equipment access and
choice of growth measurement have not been established. No experiment has been
performed and no result or preliminary data are available. A null estimate with
wide uncertainty would not establish that the schedules are equivalent.
